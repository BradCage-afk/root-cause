# INC-301 — Notification sends timing out during campaign burst

About 40% of campaign notifications failed over 75 minutes.

## Timeline
- 18:00 marketing campaign queues 2 million notifications
- 18:05 alert: notification send failures above 20%
- 18:22 workers blocked waiting for a database connection
- 19:20 campaign rate-limited, incident closed

## Contributing factors
A marketing campaign burst queued 2 million notifications. notification-worker could not acquire
database connections from pgbouncer-main: the connection pool was exhausted and sends timed out.

## Action taken
Rate-limited the campaign. Restarted workers.
