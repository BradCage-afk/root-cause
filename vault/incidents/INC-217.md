# INC-217 — Search indexing lag after batch reindex

Indexing fell about 40 minutes behind. Queries served stale results for roughly 2.5 hours.

## Timeline
- 09:02 scheduled batch reindex starts
- 09:14 alert: indexer lag above 15m
- 09:31 on-call notes indexer workers cannot acquire database connections
- 10:05 batch reindex paused; lag begins clearing
- 11:42 lag nominal, incident closed

## Contributing factors
A batch reindex started at 09:02 opened hundreds of database connections through pgbouncer-main. The connection pool was exhausted, so indexer workers could not acquire connections and indexing fell 40 minutes behind.

## Action taken
Paused the batch reindex. Restarted indexer workers.

## Follow-up
Reindex schedule staggered in [[REM-37]]. [[PLT-449]] still unscheduled.
